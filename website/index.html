Where the data comes from
Incident leads
DeFiHackLabs catalog (Apache-2.0), parsed from README entries and header identifiers, plus protocol post-mortems and original security research.
Source & chain evidence
Sourcify, Etherscan, and historical Ethereum RPC reads. Records retain addresses, transaction identifiers and source hashes.
New reports
RSS and optional Serper find unverified leads. They stay in a separate review queue; they do not automatically enter this snapshot.
What we publish
Metadata, evidence links and our own summaries. No contract source, PoCs or copied article/post bodies.
What remains unknown
Earliest disclosure, exploit-family grouping, source availability at incident time and human adjudication. Publication dates and source matches do not resolve these gaps.
