# Evaluation boundaries

Model benchmarks, training and weight downloads remain paused until explicitly
resumed. Ordinary user-requested reviews are separate from benchmark evaluation.
Historical benchmark runners and internal experiment archives are not distributed
with this toolkit. Do not claim measured detection quality from smoke fixtures,
report validation or known-answer public examples.

If evaluation is later authorized, pin the skill, model, dataset and analyzer;
compare skill-guided and bare-model reviews under the same context/output budget.
Keep chronological splits and exploit families isolated. No iteration on a sealed
test set. Exclude the target incident/family from point-in-time retrieval, and
strip PoC headers, labels, write-ups and prior predictions from all inputs.

Report every baseline B0–B4 and mark unavailable results explicitly. Preserve
unresolved adjudication, source-coverage gaps, refusals and timeouts. Report
confidence intervals per incident. AI agreement is not human verification;
proposed research records remain ineligible until separately adjudicated.
